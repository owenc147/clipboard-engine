## App and design research

Binocular is designed as a personal football scouting desk: league selection on the left, a weekly game plan in the center, and a separate evidence view for decisions. The football identity uses oversized condensed headlines, a charcoal-and-red broadcast palette, numbered scouting calls, and an original stadium illustration with seating tiers, ribbon lights and yard lines. Silver-blue scouting notes separate analysis from the darker game dashboard. Decoration stays behind the data so scores and actions remain readable.

| Research finding | Decision in Binocular |
|---|---|
| League context is essential to useful advice | Show custom scoring and verify research candidates against every roster. |
| A user needs to understand why a move helps | Put the recommendation, reasoning, uncertainty and source links together. |
| Data and analysis age at different rates | Separate league-snapshot, player-cache and research dates. Explain that Refresh does not rewrite research. |
| Small Mac windows need flexible navigation | Add a collapsible sidebar and native View menu shortcuts. |
| Football decoration can compete with dense information | Use field lines in the scoreboard; use opaque, quieter cards for text and tables. |
| Small low-contrast text is difficult to read | Increase secondary text sizes and contrast; add zoom controls, keyboard tab navigation and textual status labels. |

The navigation approach follows [Apple’s sidebar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars). Legibility and keyboard work draw on [Apple’s accessibility guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility). [FantasyPros My Playbook](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/myplaybook/intro) is a useful category reference for league-aware lineup, waiver and trade workflows; Binocular emphasizes inspectable reasons rather than a single unexplained score.

## What is implemented

- Binocular name, custom binocular app icon, football-field scoreboard and scouting-room styling.
- Game plan and Scouting report views, with dated Week 2 calls for all three leagues.
- Clickable sources that open in your browser, evidence notes and expandable conditions for revisiting each call.
- Full-roster ownership checks for the curated research shortlist, with unrostered/rostered filters.
- Reserve roster tab, detailed scoring, data timestamps and warnings when research and roster context diverge.
- Current NFL week selection, corrected adds terminology, explicit limits on roster-count trade heuristics and support for commissioner-adjusted matchup scores.
- Native refresh/view/zoom/sidebar shortcuts and saved snapshots that keep report text and metadata together.

## Next features worth building

1. **Custom-scoring projections:** obtain a permitted projection feed, apply every league’s scoring rules, then compare legal lineups. Do not substitute ordinal rankings for point forecasts.
2. **Role trends across weeks:** show routes, target shares, carries and red-zone usage with dates and sample sizes, ideally through a reliable licensed feed.
3. **Trade evaluation:** account for replacement players, lineup slots, manager needs and the value lost when a two-for-one trade forces a drop. Do not use an acceptance probability without evidence.
4. **Persistent research refresh:** an explicit research workflow could update sources and archive prior calls. The current app refreshes Sleeper data only.
5. **Broader Mac polish:** system light appearance, a fully native sidebar/toolbar, and a wider accessibility audit. The current app is a native Mac window with a local web-based interface.

## Limits and what to recheck

Only one regular-season week is available for usage analysis. Published rankings disagree, injury statuses can change, and generic projections do not match every custom scoring setting. The current calls have not been backtested as a forecasting model. No waiver bids, trades, messages or lineup changes were submitted. Recheck final practice reports, inactive lists, ownership and waiver rules in Sleeper before acting.

### Stadium design update — September 16

The requested ESPN and NFL inspiration appears in the scoreboard hierarchy, compact labels and red accents. Madden inspires the game-day atmosphere and stadium presentation; Apple inspires legible body text, consistent controls, keyboard navigation and restrained surfaces. These are design interpretations, with Binocular’s own artwork and identity.

[EA’s official presentation deep dive](https://www.ea.com/games/madden-nfl/madden-nfl-26/news/madden-26-gridiron-notes-presentation-deep-dive) describes coordinated field art, stadium ribbon graphics and primetime presentation. Binocular translates those ideas into a static stadium backdrop rather than adding distracting motion. [Apple’s typography guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) informs the separation between expressive display text and readable information.
